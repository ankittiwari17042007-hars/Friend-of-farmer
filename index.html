🌱 Why Do We Need a Friend of Farmer?

For a farmer, the field is like a son. A farmer spends months caring for the soil and crops, just as a parent cares for a child.

👨‍🌾 Treat the Soil Like We Treat a Child

When a child becomes sick, we do not simply give medicine without knowing the problem. We take the child to a doctor, find the cause of the illness, and then follow the right treatment.

In the same way, our soil also needs proper care and diagnosis. Before adding fertilizer to a field, we should first test the soil and understand its condition.

🔬 Test the Soil Before Adding Fertilizer

Soil testing helps farmers understand which nutrients are deficient or excessive and what the crop actually needs. Based on the soil test, farmers can choose the right type and quantity of fertilizer instead of applying fertilizer simply by guesswork.

1️⃣ Test the Soil ➡️ 2️⃣ Identify the Problem ➡️ 3️⃣ Understand the Crop's Need ➡️ 4️⃣ Apply the Right Nutrients

🌾 Reduce Unnecessary Chemical Use

Chemical fertilizers can be useful when they are needed and applied correctly. However, they should not be used blindly or excessively.

We should encourage the proper use of compost, farmyard manure, organic manure and bio-fertilizers wherever appropriate.

🐄 From Cow Dung to Useful Compost

Cow dung and other suitable organic materials can be converted into well-prepared compost or organic manure instead of being wasted. These organic inputs can help improve soil quality and support healthy farming when used properly.

🤝 Our Message

Don't guess. Test the soil, understand the problem, and then take the right action.

A healthy field means a healthy crop, a stronger farming future, and better care for our land.
